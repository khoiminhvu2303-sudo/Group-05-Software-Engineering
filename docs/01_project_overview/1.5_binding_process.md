# 1.6 Constraint process
**1.6.1 Primary key constraint**
 
  Each table in the database has a unique primary key of type INT, set to AUTO_INCREMENT to ensure the uniqueness of each record:
•	Publisher: PublisherID
•	Category: CategoryID
•	Author: AuthorID
•	Staff: StaffID
•	Reader: ReaderID
•	BookTitle: TitleID
•	Title_Author: Title_AuthorID
•	BookCopy: CopyID
•	LoanRecord: LoanID
•	LoanDetail: LoanDetailID
•	FineRecord: FineID

**1.6.2 Foreign key constraint**

Foreign keys are established to maintain referential integrity between tables. Specifically:
| BOARD	| FOREIGN KEY |	REFERENCE TABLE |	ON UPDATE |	ON DELETE |
|----|----|-----|-----|-----|
|BookTitle |	PublisherID |	Publisher(PublisherID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|BookTitle |	CategoryID |	Category(CategoryID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|Title_Author |	TitleID |	BookTitle(TitleID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|AuthorID |	AuthorID |	Author(AuthorID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|BookCopy |	TitleID |	BookTitle(TitleID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|LoanRecord |	ReaderID |	Reader(ReaderID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|LoanRecord |	CreatedBy | StaffID	Staff(StaffID) |	CASCADE |	SET NULL |
|LoanDetail |	LoanID |	LoanRecord(LoanID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|LoanDetail |	CopyID |	BookCopy(CopyID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|FineRecord |	LoanID |	LoanRecord(LoanID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
|FineRecord |	CollectedBy_StaffID |	Staff(StaffID) |	CASCADE |	RESTRICT |
