## 3.2 Data Flow Diagram ( DFD )

---

### 3.2.1 Data Flow Diagrams level 0 (Context Diagram)

At the macro level, process **0.0 Library Management System** serves as the core processing engine for all library operations, including book circulation, document cataloging, reader management, and reporting. The system communicates directly with 3 external entities: **Reader**, **Staff**, and **Admin**.

#### Interacting Data Flows:

* **Reader Entity:**
  * **Incoming Data Flows (Input):** `Registration Request`, `Book Reservation Request`, `Fine Payment`
  * **Outgoing Data Flows (Output):** `Membership Card`, `Borrow Receipt`, `Book Delivery`

* **Staff Entity:**
  * **Incoming Data Flows (Input):** `Barcode Scan Data`, `Book Status`, `Fine Calculation`
  * **Outgoing Data Flows (Output):** `User Authentication`, `Operational Reports Data Entry`

* **Admin Entity:**
  * **Incoming Data Flows (Input):** `System Configuration`, `Account & Permission Data`, `Report Query`
  * **Outgoing Data Flows (Output):** `Systems log & Audit Trail`, `Management & Analytical Reports`

---

### 3.2.2 Data Flow Diagrams level 1

The central system is decomposed into **7 core business processes** interacting directly with **4 primary data stores**:

#### Data Stores:
* **`D1: Catalog`**: Stores document metadata, barcodes, shelf locations, and availability statuses.
* **`D2: Reader_DB`**: Stores reader profiles, account credentials, card validity, and penalty histories.
* **`D3: Loan_Records`**: Stores loan receipts, due dates, transaction histories, and book reservations.
* **`D4: Fine_Records`**: Stores violation tickets, fine amounts, penalty reasons, and payment statuses.

#### Level 1 Process Descriptions:

1. **`1.0 Reader Management`:**
   * Receives `Registration Request` from readers.
   * Reads and updates `Account Data` and `Card Status` in **`D2: Reader_DB`**.
   * Issues `Reader Card Details` back to the reader.

2. **`2.0 Document & Inventory Management`:**
   * Receives `Book Information` entered by library staff.
   * Writes `New Book Data` into **`D1: Catalog`** and sets the status to `Active`.
   * Outputs the `Book Barcode` for staff labeling.

3. **`3.0 Search & Book Reservation`:**
   * Receives `Book Search` or `Reservation Request` from readers.
   * Queries `Book Catalog` data from **`D1: Catalog`**.
   * Returns `Search Results` to the reader and commits `Update Reservation Status` to **`D1: Catalog`** upon successful reservation.

4. **`4.0 Circulation Management`:**
   * Receives `Borrow Request` from readers and `Scan Barcode` inputs from staff.
   * Validates staff credentials via `Account Validation` and checks `Card Validity` against **`D2: Reader_DB`**.
   * Reads current `Book Status` from **`D1: Catalog`**.
   * Triggers `Issue Loan Slip` for staff, returns `Loan Receipt` to the reader, and logs `Loan Slip Details` into **`D3: Loan_Records`**.

5. **`5.0 Return & Renewal Management`:**
   * Receives returned physical items (`Return Book`) or `Return / Renewal Request` from readers.
   * Executes `Close Loan Slip` or updates the extended `Due Date` in **`D3: Loan_Records`**.
   * Restores book status to `Available` in **`D1: Catalog`**.
   * In cases of late return or item damage, logs `Overdue` status to **`D3: Loan_Records`**, passes `Violation Details` to process **6.0**, and issues a `Violation Report` to the reader.

6. **`6.0 Fine & Payment Management`:**
   * Receives `Violation Details` forwarded from process **5.0**.
   * Processes the incoming `Pay Fine` transaction from the reader.
   * Outputs `Issue Receipt` to the reader and commits `Payment Status` to **`D4: Fine_Records`**.

7. **`7.0 Staff & Report Management`:**
   * Receives `Report Query` or `Account & Permission Data` configurations from the Admin.
   * Extracts aggregated `System Data` across all four data stores: **`D1: Catalog`**, **`D2: Reader_DB`**, **`D3: Loan_Records`**, and **`D4: Fine_Records`**.
   * Generates and returns `System Reports` to the Admin.

### 3.2.3 Data Flow Diagrams level 2

---

#### 3.2.3.1 Sub-process 1.0: Reader Management

* **`1.1 Receive & Verify Information`:**
  * **Input:** `Reader Information` supplied by the user.
  * **Action:** Validates entry completeness and structural correctness.
  * **Output:** Generates a standardized `Registration Application` forwarded to process 1.2.

* **`1.2 Validate & Approve Account`:**
  * **Input:** `Registration Application` from 1.1 and `Account Information`.
  * **Action:** Verifies policy compliance, approves registration, and commits `Account Data` to **`D2: Reader_DB`**.
  * **Output:** Passes `Approved Application` to process 1.3.

* **`1.3 Issue Card & Store Data`:**
  * **Input:** `Approved Application` from 1.2 and `Card Status` retrieved from **`D2: Reader_DB`**.
  * **Action:** Initializes card parameters and sets expiration dates.
  * **Output:** Delivers complete `Reader Card Details` to the reader.

---

#### 3.2.3.2 Sub-process 2.0: Document Inventory Management

* **`2.1 Receive & Catalog Books`:**
  * **Input:** New `Book Information` submitted by staff.
  * **Action:** Catalogs document metadata (title, author, publisher, publication year).
  * **Output:** Stores `New Book Data` into **`D1: Catalog`**.

* **`2.2 Generate Barcode & Labeling`:**
  * **Input:** `Book Copy Details` queried from **`D1: Catalog`**.
  * **Action:** Generates unique barcode identifiers and assigns shelf mapping codes.
  * **Output:** Produces `Book Barcode` for physical printing and passes `Label Code & Location` to process 2.3.

* **`2.3 Save Information to System`:**
  * **Input:** `Label Code & Location` from process 2.2.
  * **Action:** Synchronizes location data with physical inventory entries.
  * **Output:** Commits `Update Inventory Data` directly into **`D1: Catalog`**.

---

#### 3.2.3.3 Sub-process 3.0: Search & Book Reservation

* **`3.1 Look Up & Search Documents`:**
  * **Input:** `Search Request` submitted by the user.
  * **Action:** Queries items based on keywords, titles, or categories.
  * **Data Store Access:** Fetches `Look Up Book Information` from **`D1: Catalog`**.
  * **Output:** Displays matching `Search Results`.

* **`3.2 Check Book Reservation Eligibility`:**
  * **Input:** `Reservation Request`.
  * **Action:** Evaluates item availability via `Check Book Status` in **`D1: Catalog`** and verifies user privileges via `Check Card Status` in **`D2: Reader_DB`**.
  * **Output:** Passes `Valid Request` to process 3.3.

* **`3.3 Create & Update Book Reservation`:**
  * **Input:** `Valid Request` from process 3.2.
  * **Action:** Creates reservation records and places a temporary hold on the title.
  * **Data Store Access:** Writes `Update Book Status` to **`D1: Catalog`** and logs `Save Reservation Slip` into **`D3: Loan_Records`**.
  * **Output:** Sends `Result Notification` to the reader.

---

#### 3.2.3.4 Sub-process 4.0: Circulation Management

* **`4.1 Check Borrowing Eligibility`:**
  * **Input:** `Book Borrowing Request` alongside `Card / Book Information`.
  * **Action:** Checks card expiration, active loans count, and unpaid fines.
  * **Data Store Access:** Executes `Check Card Expiry / Violations` against **`D2: Reader_DB`**.
  * **Output:** Forwards `Eligibility Confirmed` state to process 4.2.

* **`4.2 Create Borrow Record`:**
  * **Input:** `Eligibility Confirmed` from process 4.1.
  * **Action:** Formulates checkout records and determines due dates.
  * **Data Store Access:** Verifies `Check Book Availability / Reservation Status` in **`D1: Catalog`** and commits `Save BorrowRecord & Details` to **`D3: Loan_Records`**.
  * **Output:** Passes `Transfer Borrowing Data` to process 4.3.

* **`4.3 Update Status & Hand Over Book`:**
  * **Input:** `Transfer Borrowing Data` from process 4.2.
  * **Action:** Updates item state and prints transaction slips.
  * **Data Store Access:** Writes `Update Book Status = "Borrowed"` to **`D1: Catalog`**.
  * **Output:** Executes `Print / Hand Over Borrow Receipt & Book` to the reader.

---

#### 3.2.3.5 Sub-process 5.0: Return & Renewal Management

* **`5.1 Receive & Verify Borrowing Slip`:**
  * **Input:** `Return/Renewal Request`.
  * **Action:** Validates borrowing record details against system entries.
  * **Data Store Access:** Fetches `Borrowing Slip Details` from **`D3: Loan_Records`**.
  * **Output:** Routes execution paths to either `Renewal Request` (process 5.2) or `Return Request` (process 5.3).

* **`5.2 Process Extension`:**
  * **Input:** `Renewal Request` from process 5.1.
  * **Action:** Re-calculates return deadlines according to library policy.
  * **Data Store Access:** Writes extended `Due Date` to **`D3: Loan_Records`**.
  * **Output:** Displays `Success Notification`.

* **`5.3 Process Return & Check Violations`:**
  * **Input:** `Return Request` from process 5.1.
  * **Action:** Inspects actual return dates against target due dates and checks item condition.
  * **Data Store Access:** Writes `Update "Returned" Status` to **`D3: Loan_Records`** and resets item status to `Available` in **`D1: Catalog`**.
  * **Output:** Emits `Overdue` violation data for penalty assessment if infractions are found.

---

#### 3.2.3.6 Sub-process 6.0: Fine & Payment Management

* **`6.1 Lookup & Determine Fine`:**
  * **Input:** `Violation Details` read from **`D4: Fine_Records`**.
  * **Action:** Calculates fine amounts based on overdue days or physical damage metrics.
  * **Output:** Passes calculated `Fine Amount` to process 6.2.

* **`6.2 Process Fine Payment`:**
  * **Input:** `Fine Amount` from process 6.1 and `Pay Fine` payment input from the reader.
  * **Action:** Processes financial transactions and generates payment receipts.
  * **Output:** Issues `Issue Receipt` to the reader and sends `Payment Confirmation` to process 6.3.

* **`6.3 Update Violation Record`:**
  * **Input:** `Payment Confirmation` from process 6.2.
  * **Action:** Clears penalty flags and closes active fine records.
  * **Data Store Access:** Commits `Update Status` into **`D4: Fine_Records`**.

---

#### 3.2.3.7 Sub-process 7.0: Staff & Report Management

* **`7.1 Receive & Categorize Request`:**
  * **Input:** Incoming `Report Request`.
  * **Action:** Categorizes query types (circulation metrics, reader stats, fine history, inventory inventory).
  * **Data Store Access:** Dispatches `Query Data` requests simultaneously to **`D1: Catalog`**, **`D2: Reader_DB`**, **`D3: Loan_Records`**, and **`D4: Fine_Records`**.
  * **Output:** Forwards `Received Request` to process 7.2.

* **`7.2 Collect & Aggregate Data`:**
  * **Input:** `Received Request` from process 7.1 and raw query result sets.
  * **Action:** Consolidates, filters, and groups data parameters.
  * **Output:** Produces structured `Aggregated Data` and `Raw Data` for process 7.3.

* **`7.3 Generate Report`:**
  * **Input:** `Aggregated Data` and `Raw Data` from process 7.2.
  * **Action:** Formats dataset into standard system template views.
  * **Output:** Generates finalized `System Report` delivered to Admin.
