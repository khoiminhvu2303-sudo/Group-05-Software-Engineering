** 3.1 Identification of entities and attributes:
** 3.1.1: Design overview:
The Library Management System is designed to automate all operations related to book storage and circulation, document management, user management, and the handling of violations in a synchronized and accurate manner. The system's database is built upon a relational data model, comprising key entities categorized into three subsystem groups: Document Management, User Management, and Circulation & Violation Management. These entities are tightly linked via a system of Primary Keys (PK) and Foreign Keys (FK) to maintain referential integrity and prevent data redundancy.
** 3.1.2 List of entities
** Group 1: Document Management
** 3.1.2.1. Book Entity:
Description: Stores detailed information about each book title and document in the library to facilitate lookup, inventory management, and the processing of borrowing/returning transactions.
Attributes:
-Book ID: PK (Primary Key) – A unique identifier for each book.
-Title: The title of the book.
-Summary: A brief description of the book's content.
-Publication Year: The year the document was published.
-Stock Quantity: The number of copies currently available on the shelf.
-Status: The condition/availability of the book (Available/On Loan/Damaged). -	AuthorID: FK (Foreign Key) – References the Author table to identify the author.
-PublisherID: FK (Foreign Key) – References the Publishers table to identify the publisher.
-CategoryID: FK (Foreign Key) – References the Category table to identify the genre/category.
Design note: Linked to parent reference tables (Author, Publishers, Category) to avoid data redundancy and support multi-criteria search filters for readers.
** 3.1.2.2. Author Entity:
Description: Stores personal information and biographies of authors whose books are held in the library.
Attributes:
-AuthorID: PK (Primary Key) – Unique identifier for each author.
-AuthorName: Author's full name.
-Biography: Brief overview of the author's life and career.
Design note: Acts as a parent reference table for the Book entity. An author may have multiple books in the system (One-to-Many relationship).
** 3.1.2.3. Publishers Entity
Description: Stores information about book publishing and distribution entities.
Attributes:
-PublisherID: PK (Primary Key) – Unique identifier for the publisher.
-Name: Publisher's name.
-Address: Publisher's headquarters address.
-Contact: Contact phone number or email.
Design note: Provides reference data for the Book entity to manage the publication source of the materials.
** 3.1.2.4. Category Entity
Description: Defines the classification of book topics within the library (e.g., Information Technology, Textbooks, Literature...). 
Attributes:
-CategoryID: PK (Primary Key) - Unique identifier for the category.
-CategoryName: Name of the book category.
Design note: Supports document grouping on the Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC) system, enabling readers to easily filter and search for books.
** Group 2: User Management
** 3.1.2.5. Reader Entity
Description: Stores personal information, online login accounts, and activity status for readers registered for a library card.
Attributes:
-ReaderID: PK (Primary Key) - Unique reader identifier.
-Full name: Reader's full name.
-DOB: Date of birth.
-Phone: Contact phone number.
-Email: Personal email address.
-Address: Permanent or temporary residential address.
-Card Expiration Date: Library card expiration date.
-Username: Username for the OPAC portal.
-Password: Login password (securely encrypted).
Design note: Account attributes are integrated directly into the table, allowing readers to manage their borrowing history, renewals, or book reservations online without the need for a separate account table.
** 3.1.2.6. Admin Entity
Description: Stores identification and account information for high-level administrators with full system management privileges.
Attributes:
-AdminID: PK (Primary Key) - Unique identifier for the administrator.
-AdminName: Administrator's full name.
-Email: Official email address.
-Phone: Contact phone number.
-Username_Admin: Username for the administration system. 
-Password_Admin: Login password (encrypted).
Design note: Manages special privileges, including creating staff accounts, configuring system parameters, and generating financial or transaction reports.
** 3.1.2.7. Staff Entity
Description: Stores personal information, position, salary, and account details for library staff working directly at the service counter.
Attributes:
-StaffID: PK (Primary Key) - Unique staff identifier.
-Name_Staff: Staff member's full name.
-Gender: Male/Female.
-DOB: Staff member's date of birth.
-Phone: Personal phone number.
-Email: Contact email.
-Address: Permanent address.
-Salary: Base salary.
-Username_Staff: Login account for the management application.
-Password_Staff: Login password (encrypted).
-ManagedBy_AdminID: FK (Foreign Key) - ID of the administrator directly supervising this staff member (references AdminID in the Admin table). Design Note: The `ManagedBy_AdminID` foreign key links directly to the `Admin` table to establish a strict personnel management relationship.
** 3.1.2.8. Guest Entity
Description: Represents a transient user who has not logged into the system and performs only public searches.
Attributes (Virtual entity for business modeling purposes):
-SessionID: Temporary access session identifier.
-SearchKeyword: Keyword used for document searches.
Design Note: This entity is not represented in the actual database but is used in the design of Use-case diagrams and Data Flow Diagrams (DFD).
Group 3: Circulation and Processing
** 3.1.2.9. Borrow_Record Entity
Description: Records general information regarding a book borrowing transaction initiated at the library.
Attributes:
-TransactionID: PK (Primary Key) – Unique borrowing slip identifier.
-ReaderID: FK (Foreign Key) – References the `Reader` table to identify the borrower.
-StaffID: FK (Foreign Key) – References the `Staff` table to identify the librarian who created the slip.
-DateBorrowed: Date the reader borrowed the book.
-ReturnDate: Scheduled due date for returning the book.
Design Note: Acts as an intermediary table connecting readers and librarians. Detailed information regarding the borrowed books is separated into the `BorrowDetail` table to achieve a higher normalization level.
** 3.1.2.10. Borrow_Detail Entity (Borrowing Details)
Description: Stores specific details for each book included in a borrowing slip, facilitating the management of individual book returns and the monitoring of physical condition. Attributes:
-BorrowDetail_ID: PK (Primary Key) – Unique identifier for the borrowing detail record.
-TransactionID: FK (Foreign Key) – Links directly to the BorrowRecord entity.
-Book ID: FK (Foreign Key) – Links to the Book entity to identify the borrowed book.
-Book status: Condition of the book upon return (Intact, torn cover, missing pages, etc.).
-Actual_return_date: The actual date the reader returns the book to the library.
Design note: Separating this table ensures a high level of database normalization (3NF) and addresses the scenario where a single borrowing record may include multiple different books.
** 3.1.2.11. Fine_Receipt Entity (Fine Receipt)
Description: Records information regarding the handling of library rule violations, such as late returns, or the loss or damage of materials.
Attributes:
-FineID: PK (Primary Key) – Unique identifier for the fine receipt.
-TransactionID: FK (Foreign Key) – References the specific borrowing record associated with the violation.
-Reason: Specific reason for the fine (e.g., X days late, damaged book).
-Amount: The compensation or fine amount to be paid.
-Payment Status: Status of the fine collection (Pending / Paid).
Design note: Closely linked to the library's circulation and financial management processes; allows for the implementation of automated triggers to calculate fines based on the number of days overdue.
