**1.1  System Overview**


Amidst the rapidly growing demand for access to knowledge and information, library management and operations have become increasingly complex. The project "Analysis and Design of a Library Management System" aims to analyze the actual operational processes of a library and subsequently propose and develop an information system to comprehensively support the management of its operational activities.


The system targets three primary user groups: library management, librarians (staff), and patrons. Internally, it handles core operations such as managing patron information and book catalogs, processing loans and returns, tracking on-site reading attendance, managing fine payments, and generating statistical reports. For patrons, the system provides an interface to search for materials and track their borrowing history and book renewal status.


All the aforementioned operations are integrated into a unified platform, replacing the decentralized, manual methods—such as logbooks or spreadsheets—currently common at many facilities; this enhances operational efficiency, minimizes management errors, and improves the service experience for readers.
